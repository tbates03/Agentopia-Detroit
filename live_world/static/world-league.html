Culture is contributed — not guessed

TAi should not invent someone else's culture. A country is not one culture, and a language is not one uniform vocabulary. Holiday calendars, local workflows and culturally specific tasks should come from lived experience, professional experience, fluent/native review, community review or authoritative sources. Machine translation alone is not cultural validation.

We do not rank cultures, religions, languages or communities. We evaluate tasks, model sufficiency and system behavior.

Language-First Research

If the work is performed in Colombian Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Arabic, Korean, French, Yoruba, Hindi, Japanese or another language variety, contribute it in that language first.

Do not translate yourself into our assumptions. Teach the system how the work is actually done.

What TAi Learns

TAi can build evidence about task complexity, language, professional vocabulary, regional workflow differences, model-tier success, latency, escalation, persistence and hardware cost.

This community layer is research/evaluation first. Model-training permission is separate and explicit.
