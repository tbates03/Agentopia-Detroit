# Research Packs

Research Packs add real tasks, occupations, trades, languages, and local workflow context to the right-sizing research.

## Please use your language

If the work is performed in Spanish, submit the task in Spanish. If it is performed in Portuguese, submit it in Portuguese. If regional terminology matters, preserve it.

English summaries are welcome for maintainers, but they are not a replacement for the native-language task.

## Cultural provenance

Describe how you know the context you are submitting: lived experience, professional experience, fluent/native review, community review, authoritative sources, or a combination.

Do not invent cultural behavior for a community you do not know.

## Consent

Each pack separately states whether it may be used for public research/evaluation and whether the contributor permits future model training/fine-tuning.

Training consent is optional and defaults to false.
